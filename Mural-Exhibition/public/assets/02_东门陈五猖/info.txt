东门陈五猖
East Gate Chen Wuchang

一、 神祇名片 (Deity Identity)
尊号 (Title)：东门陈五猖
神职 (Role)：梅山神系五部猖神之一，镇守东方，司掌驱邪、镇煞与护卫。
源流 (Origin)：深植于巫楚文化与梅山法教传统，为民间信仰中不可或缺的护法武神。

二、 图像考释 (Visual & Historical Context)
在水龙祠壁画宏大的借兵图场景中，紧跟在南门何五猖之后的便是东门陈五猖。
他身着黄色衫衣，呈现出一副充满爆发力的冲刺战斗状：左手执剑凌厉地刺向前方，右手执锤用力拢起，蓄势待发。
通过与传统存世的“梅山图”进行对比考证，壁画中所绘的陈五猖在整体形象与气势上与古本差别不大，只是手中所执的兵器有所不同。这种结合了具体法器变迁的刚猛姿态，不仅凸显了猖神镇守一方的强大神力，更体现了画师对民间神祇力量感的精准捕捉，进一步印证了梅山信仰在湖南江永勾蓝瑶地区生动而鲜活的艺术演变。

三、 英文简介 (English Summary)
East Gate Chen Wuchang serves as one of the formidable Meishan Warriors, specifically assigned to guard the eastern direction.
In the sweeping troop-borrowing murals of the Shuilong Temple, he closely follows He Wuchang. Clad in a yellow garment, he is vividly depicted in a highly dynamic, charging combat stance—thrusting a sword forward with his left hand while powerfully drawing back a hammer with his right, ready to strike.
Comparative studies with traditional "Meishan Diagrams" confirm that his overall fierce representation remains consistent with ancient copies, though his specific weapons differ slightly. As the guardian of the East, his commanding presence stands not only as a powerful symbol of protection but also as a magnificent visual testament to the unique syncretism of local Yao folk beliefs and the Meishan pantheon.
