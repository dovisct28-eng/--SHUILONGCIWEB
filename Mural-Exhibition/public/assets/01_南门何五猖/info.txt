南门何五猖
South Gate He Wuchang
一、 神祇名片 (Deity Identity)
尊号 (Title)：南门何五猖
神职 (Role)：梅山神系五部猖神之一，镇守南方，司掌驱邪、镇煞与护佑。
源流 (Origin)：根植于深厚的巫楚文化与梅山法教传统。
二、 图像考释 (Visual & Historical Context)
在水龙祠壁画宏大的借兵图场景中，行进在最前面的便是南门何五猖。
他身着青黑衣，左手提着铜锣，右手拿着锣锤，呈弯腰前进的姿态。细节之处极具张力：他左手所执之锣放于右脚前，右手使劲伸向上空，锣锤高悬过顶，正做奋力敲锣状。
通过与传统存世的“梅山图”进行对比考证，壁画中何五猖左手提锣、右手执锤的动作与古本十分相似，但壁画的表现更为夸张，力量十足，仿佛能让观者身临其境地听到震耳欲聋的阵阵铜锣声。这种充满动感的刻画，不仅彰显了猖神刚猛无畏的神力，也真实记录了梅山信仰在湖南江永勾蓝瑶地区的在地化艺术演变。
三、 英文简介 (English Summary)
South Gate He Wuchang serves as one of the fierce Meishan Warriors, specifically guarding the southern direction.
Marching at the very forefront of the mural's troop-borrowing scene, he is clad in dark cyan-black garments. He strikes a highly dynamic, forward-bending pose—holding a bronze gong low to the ground with his left hand, while his right arm is forcefully stretched upward, raising a hammer high above his head in the midst of a powerful strike.
Comparative studies show that while his posture matches traditional "Meishan Diagrams," this mural's depiction is far more exaggerated and vigorous, almost making the viewer hear the deafening crash of the gong. He stands not only as a symbol of protection against evil but also as a vivid visual testament to the unique evolution of Wu-Chu culture and Yao folk beliefs.
