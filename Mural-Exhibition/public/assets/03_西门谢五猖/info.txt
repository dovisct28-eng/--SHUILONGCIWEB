西门谢五猖
West Gate Xie Wuchang

一、 神祇名片 (Deity Identity)
尊号 (Title)：西门谢五猖
神职 (Role)：梅山神系五部猖神之一，镇守西方，司掌驱邪、镇煞与集结。
源流 (Origin)：深植于巫楚文化与梅山法教传统，为民间信仰中极具号召力的护法武神。

二、 图像考释 (Visual & Historical Context)
在水龙祠壁画宏大的借兵图场景中，行进于陈五猖左上角、正吹奏着牛角的便是西门谢五猖。
他身着深红色衣服，神态极其生动：呈现出鼓口垂目、正专注吹起牛角的姿态。通过与传统存世的“梅山图”进行对比考证，壁画中谢五猖的动作与古本中“仰首奋力吹牛角”的形象高度一致，只是人物的朝向有所不同。
壁画中最令人惊艳的艺术处理在于对“声音”的刻画：牛角吹出的声音被画师巧妙地用图像化的流线表示了出来，充满动感，仿佛屡屡激昂的号角声正随风传入观者的耳朵。这种将听觉视觉化的精妙手法，不仅凸显了猖兵大阵集结时震耳欲聋的气势，更生动展现了湖南江永勾蓝瑶民间画师非凡的艺术想象力与表现力。

三、 英文简介 (English Summary)
West Gate Xie Wuchang serves as one of the formidable Meishan Warriors, specifically assigned to guard the western direction and rally the troops.
In the sweeping troop-borrowing murals of the Shuilong Temple, he is positioned to the upper left of Chen Wuchang. Clad in a dark red garment, he is vividly depicted with puffed cheeks and downcast eyes, deeply focused on blowing a bull horn. Comparative studies show that his action is highly consistent with traditional "Meishan Diagrams," differing only slightly in the direction he faces.
The most striking artistic detail in this depiction is the visualization of sound: the blare emerging from the horn is represented by dynamic, graphical flow lines, almost making the viewer hear the continuous, rousing horn blasts drifting through the wind. This magnificent detail stands not only as a symbol of mystical power but also as a striking testament to the extraordinary creativity of local Yao folk artists.
