翻坛张五郎
 Zhang Wulang
一、 神祇名片 (Deity Identity)
尊号 (Title)：张五郎
神职 (Role)：梅山祖师、猖兵统领，位居九位猖兵之绝对核心。
源流 (Origin)：梅山法教的最高信仰符号，以其独特的“翻坛倒立”形象深植于巫楚民间信仰之中。

二、 图像考释 (Visual & Historical Context)
在水龙祠壁画宏大的借兵图大阵中，张五郎稳居九位猖兵的中心位置，其形象极具视觉冲击力。
他呈现出标志性的**“翻身倒立，两腿朝天”**姿态，两手朝前敏捷地抓住一只飞鸟。细节刻画充满了野性与狂热：他头戴箍环，长发随风披飘，双目圆瞪，口中紧紧咬着一把短刀；身着耀眼的金色上衣与红色裤子，赤足（跣足）而立。

通过考证，壁画中的张五郎与民间信仰（如民间神案上供奉的“双手撑地，两脚朝天”的木雕神像）以及传统“梅山图”中的特征高度一致。这种极具张力的“翻坛倒立”不仅是其神灵身份认知的最核心标志，也是他在壁画众多人物中能够被观者一眼识别的关键缘由，完美彰显了梅山祖师的无上法力与统帅之威。

三、 英文简介 (English Summary)
Center Commander Zhang Wulang (frequently revered as "Fantan Zhang Wulang") is the Meishan Patriarch and the supreme commander of the Meishan Warriors.
Commanding the absolute center position among the nine warriors in the mural, he strikes a breathtaking and highly iconic pose: he stands completely upside down with his bare feet pointing to the sky, agilely catching a flying bird with his hands outstretched. Clad in a vibrant gold top and red pants, his fierce nature is captured through details like his headband, wild flowing hair, glaring eyes, and a short knife tightly clenched in his teeth.
Comparative studies confirm that this unique inverted ("Fantan") posture perfectly aligns with both traditional folk altar statues and ancient "Meishan Diagrams." This bold, gravity-defying representation serves as the definitive visual marker of his divine identity, making him the most instantly recognizable and captivating focal point of the entire mural.
